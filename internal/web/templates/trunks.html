Tenant egress

This is the admin side of tenant networking. Tenants build their own networks normally and connect them to a named uplink; here you decide what that uplink really is on the wire. Designate a physical NIC or bond as an external trunk, then give each tenant an uplink that egresses as a dedicated VLAN (single 802.1Q tag), QinQ (a per-tenant outer tag with the tenant's own VLANs carried inside), or a dedicated interface. Everything past the trunk — routing the tags where they belong — is your physical fabric's job. + Add tenant egress walks you through a tenant's trunk, VLAN pool, uplink and vSwitch in one go; everything below can be edited afterwards.

External trunks

A dedicated trunk (a free NIC/bond) can't also be a vSwitch uplink, bond member, or vRouter transit NIC, and can carry QinQ (which raises MTU and disables VLAN offload — original state recorded and restored on removal). A shared trunk is your management uplink itself: tenant traffic rides it as access VLANs beside untagged management, the parent is never modified, and QinQ is not available there.

This interface carries host management. It will be shared as an access trunk: tenant networks egress as single-tagged VLANs alongside untagged management, and the parent is never modified (no MTU/offload change). QinQ isn't available on a shared uplink.
